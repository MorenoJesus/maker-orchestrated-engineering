# Kanban-oriented profile

**Illustrative MOE mapping**

Represent missions as bounded work items in the existing flow. Define explicit entry, execution, review, delivery, and closure policies. Preserve local commitments and service expectations.

Limit active execution by the capacity to review and operate changes, not by available agent slots. A mission waiting for acceptance remains work in progress. Reserve capacity for incidents, uncertainty, and rework. If review queues grow, stop pulling more implementation work before adding agents.

Each item links its approved mission, delegation boundaries, current policy, candidate-bound evidence, and consequential decisions. The same record may hold all five logical contracts. Blocked items record the decision required, accountable person, and next review point.

Measure lead time distributions, age of unfinished work, review waiting time, rework, and delivered outcomes. Do not compare unlike classes of service or reward artificial ticket splitting.

**Local decisions:** commitment point, WIP limits, priority rules, acceptable review waiting time, escalation policy, required evidence, and how cancelled or superseded work is reported.
