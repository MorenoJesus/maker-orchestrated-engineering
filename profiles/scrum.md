# Scrum-oriented profile

**Illustrative; does not redefine Scrum**

Map a mission to a Product Backlog item or another bounded outcome within the team's existing process. Keep the Product Owner's business responsibilities, the Developers' engineering responsibilities, and the Scrum Master's process responsibilities intact. Maker accountability is an assignment within the team, not a new mandatory hierarchy.

Refinement establishes intent, acceptance conditions, uncertainty, and risk. Sprint Planning considers review capacity and dependencies as well as implementation work. The selected item references an authorized mission revision and applicable autonomy policy. Agents execute bounded tasks; the team remains responsible for a usable result.

The Definition of Done can reference required evidence and quality policy. Technical acceptance must not be confused with business-outcome observation. Retain normal review and retrospective practices; include agent failures, review load, costs, and policy exceptions in inspection.

Do not treat an agent as a new autonomous Product Owner or as a replacement for team accountability. Do not remove timeboxes or commitments and still claim this profile describes unchanged Scrum.

**Local decisions:** who authorizes a mission, how work maps to sprint commitments, which evidence supplements the Definition of Done, who may merge or release, and how production feedback returns to the backlog.

Source for Scrum terminology: [official Scrum Guide](https://scrumguides.org/scrum-guide.html). This document is an original MOE mapping, not a reproduction of that guide.
