# Formal-governance and regulated profile

**Illustrative control mapping; not a compliance certification**

Map MOE records to the organization's approved requirements, design, validation, change-control, and retention processes. Applicable obligations must be determined by qualified organizational owners; this framework does not select laws or attest compliance.

Retain approval identity, role, scope, time, record revision, and evidence integrity. Apply separation of duties where required. Agents may prepare validation material but must not sign as a human approver or bypass required independent review.

Use controlled baselines and authenticated decisions. Record tool and model versions when material to reproducibility or risk. Restrict sensitive data in model context, logs, caches, and external services. Evidence storage must support the required access controls, retention, holds, and retrieval procedures.

A schema field named `approved_by` is not an electronic signature system. A scan result is not a certification. A general-purpose language model's reasoning is not a substitute for a required qualified assessment.

**Local decisions:** applicable obligations, approval and signature requirements, retention and residency, validation rigor, model/provider approval, supplier controls, and release authority.
