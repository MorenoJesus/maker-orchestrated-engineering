# Mission Contract

Status: draft. This template does not authorize execution.

## Identity and ownership
Mission ID and revision; accountable human maker; outcome owner; authorization, acceptance, exception, and release authorities; applicable profile and policy revisions.

## Intent
Problem, beneficiary, intended effect, baseline, and why the work matters. Separate facts, assumptions, and open questions.

## Boundaries
In-scope and excluded behavior; resources and environments; data classification; risk and rationale; constraints; recovery considerations.

## Acceptance
Numbered, observable conditions; required checks; applicable evidence independence; explicit failure cases.

## Execution and authorization
Permitted work class; dependencies; budgets; stop and escalation conditions; decision references tied to this revision.

## Observation
Owner, measure, review date or trigger, observation window, and response if the intended effect does not occur.
