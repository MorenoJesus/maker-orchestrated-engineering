# Autonomy Policy

Status: proposal. This document is not an enforcement mechanism.

Identify the policy ID, revision, owner, affected subjects, resources, environments, data classes, effective period, and revocation mechanism. List permitted actions, denied actions, and actions requiring fresh approval. Define runtime, cost or call, retry, concurrency, and delegation-depth limits.

Map each privileged action to the host-side enforcement point, audit record, and required authority. Define credential handling, output validation, network boundaries, context access, evidence retention, and cancellation.

Define who can approve exceptions, which controls cannot be excepted, scope and expiry requirements, compensating controls, and how revoked or conflicting authority fails closed.
