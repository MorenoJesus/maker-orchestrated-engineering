# Decision Record

Record ID and revision; kind; actor and verified authority; timestamp; affected mission and policy revisions; candidate and proof-bundle revision where applicable.

State the decision as approve, reject, or defer. Explain the evidence, alternatives, rationale, scope, conditions, uncertainty, and follow-up owner. An approval applies only to the specified action and subject.

For an exception, identify the control, reason, approving authority, exact scope, expiry, compensating controls, and permitted policy basis. An agent may propose an exception but cannot grant itself one.

Preserve previous versions. A correction must not erase the historical decision.

## Communication guidance

Apply the organization's selected communication standard. ECS is this repository's default and an optional reference for adopters. Preserve exact identifiers and raw evidence. Distinguish facts, proposals, and unknowns. Use `not_run` only for known non-execution; otherwise use `unknown` when results are unavailable. See [the ECS profile](../profiles/communication-ecs.md).
