# Proof Bundle

Status: incomplete until evaluated. This is scoped evidence, not universal proof.

## Subject
Mission and policy revisions; candidate identifier and artifact digest; source and target context; relevant dependency, configuration, and environment identifiers.

## Evidence map
For each acceptance condition: required check, producer identity, actual result, run identifier, execution time, artifact reference, integrity information, limitations, and unresolved gaps. Use passed, failed, not_run, unknown, or not_applicable accurately.

## Review and exceptions
Review references, exception decision IDs and expiry, required independence, and residual risk. Preserve underlying failures. Identify any reused evidence and justify its applicability.

## Acceptance and observation
Authorized acceptance decision reference; separate delivery authorization; business-outcome observation state. Never imply an unobserved outcome has been achieved.

## Communication guidance

Apply the organization's selected communication standard. ECS is this repository's default and an optional reference for adopters. Preserve exact identifiers and raw evidence. Distinguish facts, proposals, and unknowns. Use `not_run` only for known non-execution; otherwise use `unknown` when results are unavailable. See [the ECS profile](../profiles/communication-ecs.md).
