# Delegation Contract

Status: draft. A task assignment cannot expand authority.

Record the delegation ID and revision, authorized mission and policy revisions, responsible executor, objective, permitted resources and actions, environment, inputs and immutable references, expected outputs, acceptance-condition IDs, dependencies, resource limits, deadline, retry limit, and escalation owner.

Define stop conditions for ambiguity, scope change, unavailable access, unsafe content, contradictory evidence, budget exhaustion, and cancellation. State how partial output and unresolved issues are returned. Identify which agent or human owns shared contracts and integration.
