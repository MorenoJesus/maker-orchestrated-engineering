# The MOE Manifesto

**Proposed manifesto | MOE 0.3.0 working draft**

We build software to create useful outcomes. As intelligent agents take on more engineering work, human judgment must become more deliberate, not less visible.

We value:

1. **Meaningful outcomes over generated activity.** Code, tasks, tokens, and agent counts are means, not measures of value.
2. **Human accountability over responsibility by delegation.** A maker owns technical judgment; authorized business, security, and release owners retain their decisions.
3. **Inspectable evidence over generated confidence.** We distinguish observed results, unverified claims, assumptions, and unknowns.
4. **Bounded autonomy over unrestricted execution.** Agents receive only the authority, context, resources, and time required for an approved task.
5. **Sustainable flow over maximum parallelism.** We protect review capacity, human understanding, and maintainability instead of maximizing simultaneous agents.
6. **Adaptable practice over tool allegiance.** Organizations retain their lifecycle, technologies, and governance while making human-agent collaboration explicit.
7. **Learning over automatic self-amendment.** Experience informs better practices; changes to policies, skills, and shared knowledge still require appropriate review.

The items on either side can be useful. These preferences guide tradeoffs; they do not excuse weak quality, missing documentation, or ignored organizational policy.

## Our commitment

Make intent clear. Bound the work. Preserve human understanding. Verify what can be verified. Expose uncertainty. Deliver responsibly. Learn from actual outcomes.

**Human Vision. Intelligent Execution. Verified Outcomes.**
